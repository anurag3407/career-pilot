Captain Wing

Four pilot epaulette rank stripes swept into a wing — captain, and climbing.

  • Encodes 'pilot' structurally — rank stripes, not a literal plane
  • Doubles as a rising bar chart: career growth, step by step
  • Strongest 16 px survival of all ten tested concepts (89–99/100 audit range)
  • Nothing in the resume-tools category owns four swept stripes
