# CareerPilot — Logo Design Brief (fast track)

**Name:** CareerPilot (wordmark lowercase: `careerpilot`)
**What:** AI-powered career OS — resumes, mock interviews, job tracking, portfolios, GitHub intelligence, outreach. Free & open core (MIT, built in public).
**Audience:** job seekers — students & early/mid-career tech professionals.
**Adjectives:** confident · forward-moving · guiding · smart · open
**Competitors:** resume builders, job trackers, LinkedIn tooling (category is noisy, mostly generic swooshes + blue).
**Constraints:** must survive 16 px favicon/PWA icon, dark & light UI, existing brand violet #7c3aed; product voice is a "copilot for your career".
**Current mark:** paper-plane chevron in a rounded tile (equity: minimal, pre-release).

## Assumptions
- Mark must work as a standalone app icon (rounded tile) and next to the lowercase wordmark.
- One colour must work (README, GitHub, embroidery-grade simplicity).

| 7 | Motion — single contrail stroke | **Loop C**: a C whose contrail does a loop-the-loop |

| 9 | Heritage — aviation roundel | **Roundel**: ring, horizon, rising dot |
| 10 | Paradox — negative space | **Lift**: two wings; the arrow between them is the lift |
