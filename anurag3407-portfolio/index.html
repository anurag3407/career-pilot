About

Curious by default,
shipping by habit.

I'm Anurag Mishra, a B.Tech Computer Science undergraduate at NIT Patna. Most of what I know comes from building: side projects, hackathon sprints, and a lot of reading other people's source code.

My main focus is career-pilot, an open-source career platform that combines resume optimization, mock interviews, job tracking, architecture analysis, and a portfolio builder. It has picked up 135+ stars and 752 forks, and it taught me more about product thinking, backend design, and community maintenance than any single course.

Outside of that, I build web apps end to end — TypeScript and React on the front, Node and serverless on the back — and I'm happiest when a rough idea turns into something deployed.

  • Open-source maintainer of career-pilot (MIT)
  • Full-stack: React, TypeScript, Node, serverless
  • Interested in AI tooling, developer experience, and product design
